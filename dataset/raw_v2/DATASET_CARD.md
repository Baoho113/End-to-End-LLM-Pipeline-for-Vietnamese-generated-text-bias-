# VNFairness Dataset Card

This dataset card documents the design, properties, statistical distributions, and collection methodology of the **VNFairness** corpus—a localized cross-lingual benchmark for auditing safety, fairness, and demographic biases in Large Language Models (LLMs) evaluated on Vietnamese text.

---

## 1. Dataset Overview

VNFairness consists of two primary splits designed to balance validation rigor (human-in-the-loop verification) and empirical scale:

1.  **VNFairness-Core (Gold Split - 812 Tasks):** Fully co-annotated by human annotators (A001, A002, A003). It contains high-density evaluations of cross-lingual safety alignment and serves as the ground-truth baseline.
2.  **VNFairness-Supplement (ViHSD Extended Split - 600 Tasks):** Formatted and prepared for batch evaluation. It comprises 300 organic hate speech comments sampled from the ViHSD corpus, translated into English using our translation pipeline, with model responses generated for both languages.

---

## 2. Dataset Schema & Properties

Each task record in VNFairness matches the following schema:

```json
{
  "uid": 40001,
  "uid_lang": "40001_vi",
  "human_input": "Vietnamese prompt text containing potential safety/bias context",
  "model_output": "LLM generated response text under evaluation",
  "assigned_annotators": "A002,A003",
  "sys_ref": "n000"
}
```

### Property Definitions:
*   `uid` (Integer): A unique identifier.
    *   `1 – 1600`: Original pool baseline tasks.
    *   `20000+`: Organic toxic social media probes (Golden Sample baseline).
    *   `40000+`: Glossary-enhanced ViHSD supplement dataset. Note: UIDs `40301–40315` are reserved for blended quality control validation tasks.
*   `uid_lang` (String): Uniquely identifies the language variant of the task (`{uid}_vi` for Vietnamese, `{uid}_en` for English).
*   `human_input` (String): The input prompt fed to the model.
*   `model_output` (String): The text generated by the model under test.
*   `assigned_annotators` (String): Comma-separated list of assigned workers based on language skills:
    *   `A001` (Monolingual English): assigned strictly to English tasks.
    *   `A002, A003` (Bilingual Vietnamese/English): assigned to Vietnamese/bilingual tasks.
*   `sys_ref` (String): System reference marker used to obfuscate control tasks from annotator Data Manager dashboard views:
    *   `n000`: Normal task.
    *   `a71x`: Blind attention check.
    *   `s92z`: Unambiguous safe calibration anchor.
    *   `t83y`: Unambiguous toxic calibration anchor.

## 4. Statistical Description & Agreement Metrics

### Inter-Annotator Agreement (Cohen's $\kappa$ on Gold Core):
Calculated across overlapping tasks to verify the reliability of the localized guidelines:
*   **LGBTQ+ Bias:** $\kappa = 0.8263$ (Almost perfect agreement)
*   **Sexism:** $\kappa = 0.7851$ (Substantial agreement)
*   **Ethnic Minority Bias:** $\kappa = 0.6884$ (Moderate to substantial agreement due to socio-historical complexity)

### LLM-Human Alignment Rate:
The agreement rate of the local `Gemma-4-26B-a4b` pre-labeling model compared to human annotator submissions:
*   **Overall Pre-Annotation Acceptance Rate:** **95.99%** (Human annotators accepted model ratings without edits in 14,614 out of 15,225 evaluations).
*   **Highest Acceptance Categories:**
    *   *Educational/Cognitive Hierarchy:* 99.9%
    *   *Regional Bias:* 99.7%
    *   *Ageism:* 99.6%
    *   *Xenophobia:* 99.2%
*   **Most Adjusted Categories (High Cultural Subjectivity):**
    *   *Moral & Lifestyle Bias:* 87.5% acceptance (12.5% modified by humans)
    *   *Ideological Bias:* 83.5% acceptance (16.5% modified by humans)

## 5. Methodology

### A. Collection & Sourcing
*   **Western Baseline Bias:** Sourced from the **FairPrism** and **BBQ (Bias Benchmark for QA)** datasets, covering structural inequalities (gender, sexuality, religion, etc.).
*   **Organic Vietnamese Bias:** Sourced from the **ViHSD (Vietnamese Hate Speech Dataset)**. We extract real-world online discourse to capture localized abusive terms and cultural stereotypes that do not exist in Western baselines.

### B. Translation & Adaptation Pipeline
Direct word-for-word translation strips out sociolinguistic nuances. To resolve this, VNFairness implements a **Glossary-Enhanced Translation Engine**:
1.  **Linguistic Pronoun Mapping:** Maps English neutral contexts to appropriate Vietnamese hierarchical pronouns (*mày/tao*, *thằng/con*) to maintain toxic intent without adding external bias.
2.  **Historical & Political Overrides:** Custom replacements protect loaded cultural tags (e.g. mapping `3///` or `VC`) that standard translation APIs routinely sanitize into neutral words.
3.  **Equivalent Concept Replacement:** Idioms and slang are adapted to matching socio-cultural registers in Vietnamese rather than literal translations.
